Featherline 是一款为长期使用激素疗法的人精心设计的用药记录工具，帮你把个人记录保留在自己手中。

你记录的所有内容——用药、计划、个人备注——都保存在设备本地的加密数据库中，由你掌控。

贴合真实节奏的日常记录

按你的方案建立每日或每周用药安排。在预定时间收到提醒，提供立即记录、稍后记录或在计划外服用时添加备注等快捷操作。你的方案，你的记录，你的节奏。

直观呈现你的日常

Featherline shows you how your dose timing flows across the day and week — visualizations help you see patterns in your routine. Set your own personal target ranges, mark your doses on a timeline, and check changes over the past 24 hours when you want a closer look. You can also record personal test results, and Featherline can calibrate the visualization to them, route by route, so it stays meaningful to you.

为隐私而设计

- 本地加密存储——数据默认保存在你的设备
- 可选的应用锁，支持生物识别或设备密码
- 可选的截图与最近任务内容隐藏
- 由你掌控的加密备份文件——可用于迁移到新设备或保留外部副本

主要功能

- Custom medication schedules, daily or weekly
- Smart reminders with batch logging
- Routine visualization
- Calibration to your own test results, with a prediction interval on the chart
- Month-view history with personal summaries
- Personal record logging with built-in and custom fields
- Set your body weight for more personalized visualization
- Home screen widget — see your next dose and mark it taken right from your home screen
- Journal date widget and home-screen shortcuts with running day counts
- Medication stock tracking — keep count of what you have on hand, with low-stock reminders and a runway estimate so you can reorder before you run out
- Daily journal — note how any day went and track meaningful dates with milestones

关于 Featherline

Featherline 是一款个人记录工具，不是医疗设备。所有可视化都基于你记录的时间和数量，使用公开模型计算得出，旨在帮助你更好地组织和了解自己的日常。任何健康判断或解读，请咨询你的医疗专业人士。
